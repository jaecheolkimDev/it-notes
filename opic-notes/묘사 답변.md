1\. 자주 가는 공원 묘사
--------------
```
who : 누구랑 공원을 가는지?
    A : I usually go to my favorite park with my best friends.
when    : 언제 가세요?
    A : I normally go there on the weekends
    A : Sometimes, I go to the park on the weekdays after work or school
what    : 공원에서 뭐 하세요?
    A : I always run at the park. running is very good for my health
```

```
서론  : Wow, I love your question. this question is so easy

       I like watching tv because it's very fun
```
